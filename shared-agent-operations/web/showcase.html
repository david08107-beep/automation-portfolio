    VIABILITY

    Useful pattern, unproven business.

    The review-first approach could help teams use AI for preparation while retaining human accountability. A sellable version still needs customer discovery, production identity, connected systems, shared storage, retention controls, and measured outcomes.

    HONEST BOUNDARY

    Prototype evidence, not production claims.

    This presentation is illustrative, not the live workflow. It uses fictional data and fixed campaign samples. No real email, calendar, publishing account, customer outcome, or public production deployment is claimed.
